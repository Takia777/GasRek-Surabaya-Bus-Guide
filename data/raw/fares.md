# Fare rules

Retrieved 21 November 2026.

| Rule | Value |
| --- | --- |
| General fare | Rp5.000 |
| Student fare (pelajar and mahasiswa, including S2 and S3) | Rp2.500, with a student card or school uniform |
| Elderly | Free, minimum age 60 |
| Also free | Children under 5, people with disabilities, veterans |
| Validity | One ticket is valid for 2 hours |
| Transfer | Changing to another route within 2 hours needs no new payment; the ticket is shown to the officer |
| Operators covered | Suroboyo Bus, Wira Wiri Suroboyo, Trans Semanggi Suroboyo (R2/K2L) |
